Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert both ways?

Yes — downconvert (new → old) and upconvert (old → new). Any version to any version.

Are original files safe?

Absolutely — Retro automatically creates a backup before conversion. Originals are never touched.

Mac support?

Installer is Windows‑only. Mac users can use raw extension files directly from GitHub.

Is it really free?

100% free forever. Open source. No locked features, no hidden costs, no subscriptions.
